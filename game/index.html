AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY
SEVEN JOBS · ONE SUNSET · EVERY COP IN THE CITY

Rico Santana rolls into Costa Verde at golden hour with an empty wallet and a reputation to build. Fixer Lena Cruz, garage boss Sal Bianchi, boat captain Mateo Reyes and one cop who shouldn't be helping him — Officer Dana Ruiz — have seven jobs that will make him rich, or busted.
